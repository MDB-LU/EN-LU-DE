Future Intentions

Sie haben sowohl die Seiten zum will future und zum going to future angeschaut, oder wissen bereits, wie die Zukunftsformen funktionieren. Wissen Sie aber auch, dass wir beide Formen nutzen können, um über Pläne in der Zukunft zu sprechen?

Beispiele:

I am going to visit my grandmother next week.

You don't want to cook? That's ok, I will do it.


Beide Sätze handeln von der Zukunft, aber Sie beide haben eine andere Form. Auf dieser Seite geht es darum zu erfahren, wann welche Form verwendet wird.


Will Future

Wir brauchen das will future wenn wir eine spontane Entscheidung fällen.

Beispiele:

Who wants to take notes? I will do it.

The phone is ringing. I will pick it up.

Someone's knocking. I'll open the door.


Going to Future

Wir brauchen das going to future, um über definitive Pläne in der Zukunft zu reden.

Beispiele:

I'm going to watch the match tomorrow.

They are going to leave the country soon.

She's going to buy this car.


Sie wissen nun, wann welche Form gebraucht wird. Testen Sie Ihr Wissen anhand der folgenden Übungen.

Exercise 3:

Wählen Sie den korrekten Satz aus.



A: "The suitcase is so heavy." B: "Give it to me. I will carry it."
 A: "The suitcase is so heavy." B: "Give it to me. I am going to carry it."

"I bought some new sunglasses because I will go to the beach next summer."
 "I bought some new sunglasses because I'm going to the beach next summer."

A: "Jim is back from holiday." B: "Is he? I'll give him a call."
 A: "Jim is back from holiday." B: "Is he? I'm going to give him a call."

A: "What are you doing tonight?" B: "We'll see a play at the theatre."
 A: "What are you doing tonight?" B: "We're going to see a play at the theatre."

"You can tell me your secret. I won't tell anyone."
 "You can tell me your secret. I'm not going to tell anyone."

"Congratulations! I hear you will have a baby."
 "Congratulations! I hear you're going to have a baby."

A: "I need to post these letters." B: "I'm going shopping soon. I'll post them for you."
 A: "I need to post these letters." B: "I'm going shopping soon. I'm going to post them for you."
